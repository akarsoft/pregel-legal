Pregel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Pregel is a puzzle game: draw every line of a figure once without lifting the pen. This policy says what happens to what you do in it.

What stays with you

Everything you do in Pregel stays with you: the figures you drew, your marks, your streak and freezes, your progress through the Journey, your paper and your settings are kept on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud. Pregel has no account, no server of its own and no advertising. Unless you choose to share how you play (below), we do not receive your play and we cannot see it.

What leaves your phone

  • iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Pregel keeps a copy of your play in your private iCloud database, so your streak and drawings follow you to a new iPhone. It is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; only you can read it, and we cannot. You can turn it off in the iPhone's Settings, under your name, then iCloud.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, Pregel sends your longest streak, the number of Dailies you drew and drew clean, and the achievements you earn to Game Center, Apple's service for games. Other players may see your Game Center name and scores on the leaderboards, as your Game Center settings allow. Pregel never asks you to sign in to it.
  • Purchases. Pregel+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and billing, and we see no payment details. To check that a purchase is valid and to count trial starts, renewals and refunds, Pregel uses RevenueCat, a service for in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID it makes up, the Apple purchase receipt, the product that was bought and basic device data such as the app version, the iOS version, the device model and the country of your App Store account. It does not receive your name, your email or anything you drew; it is kept under that random ID, not your name, and it is not used to track you or to show you ads. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • How you play, only if you say yes. After the first figures, Pregel asks once whether you will share how you play. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing if you say no. If you say yes, Pregel sends to Amplitude, an analytics service, in its European Union data center: when you open and leave the app, which screens you visit, which figures you open, finish, reset or leave, how long they take, the hints you use, when the Pregel+ page is shown and whether a trial starts. It also sends recordings of Pregel's own screens while you use it (what you tap and draw, never other apps or anything outside Pregel), and Amplitude works out your approximate city from your internet address. These are kept under the same random app user ID RevenueCat uses, so we can see play and purchases together; they are linked to that ID, not to your name or email, and are never used to track you across other apps or to show you ads. We use them only to decide what to make and fix next. You can change your answer any time in Pregel's Settings, under About, "Share how you play". Pregel never asks a player whose age range shows them under 13, and sends nothing for them. Amplitude's own policy is at amplitude.com/privacy.
  • Sharing. The share card of a finished figure is made on your phone. It leaves only when you share it.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. No advertising identifier. No sale of any data.

Notifications

The Daily reminder and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled on your phone. Pregel asks before sending any, and you can turn each off in Settings.

Your control

Deleting the app removes everything Pregel kept on your phone. To have the analytics kept under your app user ID deleted, write to us at the address below. The copy in your iCloud can be deleted in the iPhone's Settings, under your name, then iCloud, then Storage. Pregel+ stays with your Apple Account and comes back with Restore.

Children

Pregel does not ask a player its age range shows under 13 to share how they play, and sends no analytics for them. Apart from that, Pregel collects no personal information from children.
