Pregel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pregel is a puzzle game: draw every line of a figure once without lifting the pen. This policy says what happens to what you do in it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in Pregel stays on your iPhone: the figures you drew, your marks, your streak and freezes, your progress through the Journey, your paper and your settings. Pregel has no account, no server of its own and no advertising. We do not receive your play and we cannot see it.

What leaves your phone

  • Purchases. Pregel+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and billing, and we see no payment details. To check that a purchase is valid and to count trial starts, renewals and refunds, Pregel uses RevenueCat, a service for in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID it makes up, the Apple purchase receipt, the product that was bought and basic device data such as the app version, the iOS version, the device model and the country of your App Store account. It does not receive your name, your email or anything you drew, it is not linked to you, and it is not used to track you or to show you ads. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Sharing. The share card of a finished figure is made on your phone. It leaves only when you share it.

What we do not do

No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No sale of any data.

Notifications

The Daily reminder and the reminder before a free week ends are scheduled on your phone. Pregel asks before sending any, and you can turn each off in Settings.

Your control

Deleting the app removes everything Pregel kept on your phone. Pregel+ stays with your Apple Account and comes back with Restore.

Children

Pregel collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes.
