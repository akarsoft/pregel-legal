Help with Pregel for iPhone.

Contact

Write to support@akars.me. Tell us the iPhone model, the iOS version and what you saw.

Common questions

How do I play?

Draw every line of the figure once, without lifting your finger. To take a line back, draw back along it. A new Daily figure comes every day, the same for everyone.

My purchase does not show on another device

Open Settings in Pregel, then Restore under Pregel+. Use the same Apple Account that bought Pregel+.

How do I cancel Pregel+?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pregel+. Your marks, streak and Journey progress stay.

When does my free week end?

Pregel shows the date in Settings under Pregel+, and reminds you two days before, unless you turned the reminder off there.

The reminder does not arrive

Check that notifications are on for Pregel in the iPhone's Settings, then Notifications, and that the reminder is on in Pregel's Settings.

I missed a day

A freeze keeps your streak when you have one. If not, the streak starts again; your marks and drawings stay.
